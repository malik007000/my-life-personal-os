# ◈ MY LIFE — Your life. Your space. Your progress.

**MY LIFE** is a personal life management system designed to bring your daily activities, goals, habits, finances, knowledge and productivity into one organized workspace.

The vision is to build more than a productivity application: a personal operating system powered by AI that understands your routines, analyses your progress, helps you make better decisions and assists you in managing everyday life.

<p align="center">
  <strong>Organize your life. Understand your progress. Build your future.</strong>
</p>

---

## ✨ Overview

MY LIFE combines personal organization, productivity tracking and intelligent assistance in one application.

Instead of switching between multiple applications to manage different aspects of life, MY LIFE provides a unified environment to organize, track and understand your personal progress.

The long-term vision is to integrate an AI assistant powered by OpenAI that can understand personal context, analyse application data and execute authorized actions.

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

MY LIFE is designed as a private personal workspace.

Privacy and security priorities include:

- Authenticated access.
- User-specific data ownership.
- Secure backend operations.
- Protected API credentials.
- Safe database operations.
- Personal data privacy.

Never commit API keys, passwords, authentication tokens or private personal records to GitHub.
